🚀 About TechFest 2K26

🌟 TechFest 2K26 is a technical festival that brings together students, innovators, and technology enthusiasts to showcase their talents and creativity.

💡 What is TechFest?

🎯 TechFest features exciting competitions, workshops, and interactive sessions that inspire learning and innovation.

🏆 Objectives

  • 💻 Enhance programming and technical skills.
  • 🚀 Encourage innovation and creativity.
  • 🤝 Promote teamwork and collaboration.
  • 📚 Learn emerging technologies.
  • 🌐 Connect with students from different colleges.

🎓 Who Can Participate?

👨‍🎓👩‍🎓 Students interested in coding, web development, AI, technology, and innovation are welcome to participate.

💻 Coding Contest | 🎨 Web Design | 🧠 Technical Quiz | 📄 Paper Presentation | 🏅 Exciting Prizes
